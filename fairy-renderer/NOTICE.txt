This is an adapted renderer, not an unmodified vendor snapshot.
- motion.ts, follow.ts and animator.ts bridge to Bend motion/history modules.
- volume.ts uses the authored Fairy sphere and circular filament sections.
- dom/mount.ts keeps full display cadence at small editor sizes.
- Example characters and the upstream motion implementation are not included.
- Projection, section rendering, canvas painting, pointer and clock plumbing remain upstream.

Fairy's silhouette is the approved Gitmon-inspired authored asset in FAIRY.bend.
